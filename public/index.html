Your AI token usage, as an API.

Stored in your GitHub. Always and forever free.

npx @bicrick/tokentracker init

Or ask your agent: Install tokentracker for me.

Open source, no account. Only token counts leave your machine.

Usage over time.

      
By day, week or month. Split by tool or model.

      
GET /api/v1/users/bicrick/usage?range=ytd&group=month&split=tool

      

    


    

      
Every model you've used.

      
      
GET /api/v1/users/bicrick/models?range=ytd

      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Across every tool.
    
      
    Added up across all your machines.
    
      
    GET /api/v1/users/bicrick/tools?range=ytd
